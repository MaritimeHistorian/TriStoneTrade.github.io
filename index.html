Tri-Stone Trade Publications

Exploring trade in the Pacific world through history and lived experience

Tri-Stone Trade Publications explores the culture, history, and community of the global transportation industry, with a focus on the networks connecting Northern California with markets across the Pacific. Current work examines the evolution of international trade, from the Port of Oakland’s leading agricultural exports to San Francisco’s modern AI tech economy, bringing historical research and firsthand industry perspectives together to understand how the Bay Area connects with the world.
