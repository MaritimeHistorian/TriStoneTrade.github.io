About Tri-Stone

Tri-Stone Trade was founded in 2026 by Pierce McDonnell (孟英磊), a sixth-generation San Franciscan, to connect Northern California's food producers with buyers in China.

Pierce grew up in San Francisco and was educated at the Chinese American International School. He later served as the Chang Lan Fellow at the China Maritime Museum in Shanghai, researching the history of U.S.–China maritime trade, and spent a year in Taipei studying Chinese literature and writing for a Chinese-language magazine.

His career began in steamship and freight forwarding under industry veteran Jon Monroe. From 2023 to 2026 he was Trade Manager for U.S. Exports at Neptune Pacific / The Wonderful Company, running exports on the California–Pacific Islands service.

Trade runs in the family. One great-grandfather started a San Francisco shipping company during the Great Depression; another farmed almonds in California. Tri-Stone brings those two threads, California agriculture and Pacific shipping, together.

Pierce serves on the board of the Pacific Transportation Association, where he led planning for Steamship Night 2026.
