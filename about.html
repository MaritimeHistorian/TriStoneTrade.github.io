About Tri-Stone

Friends walk together for a lifetime; those days will not come again. One word, one life, a lifelong bond, one cup of wine. — “Friends” by Emil Wakin Chau

For generations, the Pacific has been bridged across shared tables. In an ever-changing world, with new challenges and uncertainties, we believe in the enduring power of a simple dinner to bridge differences and create a sense of common ground.

Tri-Stone Trade draws inspiration from the people who foster these connections through the bonds of food and community. We look to history for lessons for the future, finding new ways to strengthen the ties between people through trade and the shared bonds of culture.

About the Founder

Tri-Stone Trade was founded in 2026 by Pierce McDonnell (孟英磊), a sixth-generation San Franciscan, to connect America’s food producers with buyers in China. He began his career at a Chinese e-commerce shipping startup during the pandemic-era shipping crisis, when port congestion and record freight rates upended transpacific trade. From 2023 to 2026, he served as Trade Manager for U.S. Exports at Neptune Pacific, the ocean shipping division of The Wonderful Company.

Pierce serves on the board of the Pacific Transportation Association, where he has led planning for industry events, including Steamship Night banquet. He was a Chang Lan Fellow at the China Maritime Museum in Shanghai, researching the history of U.S.–China maritime trade, and studied Chinese literature in Taipei. His writing includes peer-reviewed historical scholarship for the University of California and contributions to Chinese-language publications. He is the author of J. B. Stetson: The Story of a Ship (2016) and is currently writing Steamship Night, scheduled for publication in 2027.
