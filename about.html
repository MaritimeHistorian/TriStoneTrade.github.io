About

Pierce G. McDonnell & Tri-Stone Trade Publications

My name is Pierce G. McDonnell (孟英磊). Tri-Stone Trade Publications is a personal project that engages with the evolving dynamics of trade. I am particularly interested in building stronger bridges across the Pacific world, among companies, institutions, and professional organizations.

The Name “Tri-Stone”

磊

“Tri-Stone” refers to my Chinese name, 磊 (Lěi), a character formed by three stones stacked together. It conveys a sense of candor and openness, as in the phrase guāng míng lěi luò (光明磊落, “clear and upright”). That spirit reflects how I approach this project as an open effort to connect people and build lasting relationships across the Pacific.
