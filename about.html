The Name “Tri-Stone”

磊

My name is Pierce G. McDonnell (孟英磊). “Tri-Stone” refers to my Chinese name, 磊 (Lěi), a character formed by three stones stacked together. It conveys a sense of candor and openness, as in the phrase guāng míng lěi luò (光明磊落, “clear and upright”). That spirit reflects how I approach this project as an open effort to connect people and build lasting relationships across the Pacific.
