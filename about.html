About Pierce McDonnell

Pierce McDonnell (孟英磊) is a sixth-generation San Franciscan and former Chang Lan Fellow at the China Maritime Museum in Shanghai, where he researched U.S.–China maritime trade history. He is the author of J. B. Stetson: The Story of a Ship (2016), and has written peer-reviewed history for the University of California and Chinese-language publications.

His work draws on a background in the maritime and logistics industries. Pierce serves on the board of the Pacific Transportation Association and helped plan its Steamship Night banquet.

A great-grandfather’s shipping company during the Depression first sparked Pierce’s interest in maritime history.

Tri-Stone Trade is Pierce’s independent writing and research project on Pacific maritime trade and its history.
