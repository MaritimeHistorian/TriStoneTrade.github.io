Why California

California agriculture is a family business, and much of it has been for generations. The orchards, vineyards and fields of Northern California are often run by the grandchildren and great-grandchildren of the people who planted them, and the same is true of the packers, truckers and shippers who move their harvest. Those generations have always looked west, and California’s harvests have crossed the Pacific since the days of the Gold Rush. That legacy shapes how we do business here: long-term relationships, a reputation that gets handed down, and a stake in getting it right. When you buy from California, you’re buying from people who plan to be here for the next generation too.

About the Founder

Tri-Stone Trade was founded in 2026 by Pierce McDonnell (孟英磊), a sixth-generation San Franciscan, to connect America’s food producers with buyers in China. He began his career at a Chinese e-commerce shipping startup during the pandemic-era shipping upheaval, when port congestion and record freight rates upended transpacific trade. From 2023 to 2026, he served as Trade Manager for U.S. Exports at Neptune Pacific, the ocean shipping division of The Wonderful Company. Pierce serves on the board of the Pacific Transportation Association, where he has led planning for industry events, including the Steamship Night banquet.
