Why California

California’s position on the Pacific has made it a meeting point for maritime commerce for generations. During the Gold Rush, ships carried people and goods to San Francisco from across the ocean, helping establish the port as a gateway between the American West and Asia.

As agriculture developed, California crops—including almonds and other produce—joined the movement of goods across the Pacific. These journeys linked farms and ports to distant markets through the ships, dockworkers and maritime communities that made the routes possible. Related coverage at China Daily Asia.

Ports on the Pacific

The San Francisco and Oakland waterfronts anchor distinct chapters in the region’s maritime story. Their changing harbors offer a way to trace how infrastructure, cargo and the people who move it have shaped California’s connections across the ocean.
