Bring your products to China

Tri-Stone works with small and mid-sized Northern California agricultural and consumer food brands that want to sell in China but don't have the contacts, language or time to find the right partners on their own.

Who we work with

  • Growers and packers of California agricultural products
  • Consumer packaged food and beverage brands
  • Producers exploring China for the first time, or looking for a better channel than the one they have

How we help

Buyer introductions. We connect you with retailers, importers and distributors in China and attend major trade events, including CIIE in Shanghai.

Channel guidance. We help you weigh the options, from traditional import and retail to cross-border e-commerce, and pick the one that fits your brand and scale.

Bridging both sides. We speak Mandarin and understand shipping, so we can keep conversations moving between your team and buyers in China.

How it starts

A conversation. Tell us about your products, capacity and goals for China.

A fit check. We give you an honest view of how your products might land and which channels make sense.

Introductions. Where there's a fit, we bring you and the right buyers together.

Heading to Shanghai in November

We'll be meeting buyers at the China International Import Expo. If you'd like your products in those conversations, reach out now.
